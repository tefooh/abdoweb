# Elfekky – Full Branding Identity

**From none to the universe**

---

## 1. Brand Overview

Elfekky represents clarity, intelligence, and precision. The identity is built on simplicity and modern minimalism, reflecting a forward-thinking AI driven product without relying on cliché AI visuals.

---

## 2. Art Direction

### Art Style Type

**Minimalist Line Art Illustration**

#### Characteristics:

* Clean, sharp vector lines
* Monoline strokes (consistent thickness)
* No shading or complex textures
* Abstract yet human centered visuals
* Geometric balance and symmetry

#### Usage:

* Avatars and brand mascots
* UI illustrations
* Icons and visual storytelling elements

---

## 3. Typography System

### Primary Font

**McLaren**

* Use for: Headings, titles, hero text
* Style: Bold, expressive, modern
* Should dominate visual hierarchy

### Secondary Font

**Merge One**

* Use for: Body text, descriptions, UI labels
* Style: Clean, readable, minimal
* Supports long form readability

---

## 4. Color System

### Theme Support

* Light theme (default)
* Dark theme (fully supported)

---

### Gradients

#### Primary Gradient

* From: `#DDFDFF`
* To: `#B7CEFF`

#### Secondary Gradient

* From: `#93CCEB`
* To: `#6DA1E0`

---

### Color Distribution Rule

* **70% Monochrome**

  * Black, white, greys
* **30% Gradients**

  * Used for highlights, accents, and focus areas only

---

### Monochrome Palette

* Pure Black: `#000000`
* Soft Black: `#111111`
* Dark Grey: `#333333`
* Medium Grey: `#777777`
* Light Grey: `#CCCCCC`
* White: `#FFFFFF`

---

## 5. Logo Usage

* Logos are stored in the `/assets` folder
* Always maintain clear space around the logo
* Do not distort, rotate, or recolor outside brand palette
* Prefer monochrome versions for most use cases
* Use gradient versions only in highlight contexts

---

## 6. Layout & Composition

### Core Principles

* Strong use of whitespace
* Grid based alignment
* Visual balance over decoration

### Spacing

* Generous padding and margins
* Avoid clutter at all costs

---

## 7. UI & UX Guidelines

### Design Philosophy

* Clean and distraction free
* Functional over decorative
* Precision in every element

### Components

* Flat elements only
* No shadows
* No skeuomorphic effects

---

## 8. Animation Guidelines

### Allowed

* Subtle fades
* Smooth transitions
* Micro-interactions (hover states, opacity changes)

### Not Allowed

* Bouncy animations
* Overly dynamic motion
* Distracting transitions

---

## 9. Iconography

* Line based icons only
* Consistent stroke width
* Minimal detail
* Matches illustration style

---

## 10. What to Avoid

* No shadows
* No gradients overuse
* No AI clichés (brains, circuits, glowing effects)
* No heavy textures
* No visual noise

---

## 11. Brand Personality

* Minimal
* Intelligent
* Calm
* Precise
* Future oriented

---

## 12. Summary

Elfekky’s identity is built on clarity and restraint. Every element must feel intentional, lightweight, and modern. The system relies on balance: monochrome dominance with controlled gradient accents, paired with clean typography and structured layouts.

The result is a brand that feels powerful without trying too hard.
